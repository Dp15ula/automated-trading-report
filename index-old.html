📋 Executive Summary

⚡ One-Sentence Summary

"Stay fully invested in a concentrated basket of elite tech stocks during good times, but automatically switch to protection mode when RSI or moving average danger signals appear."

📊 What is "A Better QQQ"?

QQQ is a popular ETF that owns all 100 stocks in the Nasdaq-100 index. The strategy creator's thesis: "Why dilute returns across 100 companies when a handful of mega-caps drive most of the gains?"

"A Better QQQ" concentrates on the 7 highest-conviction tech stocks plus TQQQ (3x leveraged QQQ) for amplified exposure:

TQQQ (3x) AAPL MSFT NVDA AMZN TSLA AMD TSM

Equal-weighted = 12.5% each. TQQQ adds leverage; individual stocks avoid leveraged ETF decay on 87.5% of the portfolio.

🎯 Strategy Logic Overview

The algorithm monitors RSI and moving averages to dynamically shift between modes:

  • Bull Market Default: Hold "A Better QQQ" basket
  • Oversold (RSI < 31): Concentrate into TQQQ for bounce
  • Overbought (RSI > 75-80): Hedge with UVXY volatility
  • Bear Market (SPY < 200 MA): Rotate to SQQQ, BSV, or sector ETFs

The goal: Stay fully invested during uptrends, but automatically protect capital when technical danger signals appear.

📚 Reference: Holding TQQQ in the "A Better QQQ" Basket

A common concern: Is it safe to hold TQQQ for weeks or months? The short answer: Yes, within this strategy's design — but understanding why is important.

Understanding Volatility Decay (Beta Slippage)

TQQQ promises 3x the DAILY return of QQQ. Over multiple days, compounding math creates "decay" in choppy markets:

Market Type QQQ Day 1 QQQ Day 2 QQQ Total TQQQ Total Impact
Choppy/Sideways +5% -5% -0.25% -2.25% ⚠️ Decay hurts (9x worse, not 3x)
Trending Up +2% +2% +4.04% +12.36% ✅ Compounding helps (better than 3x)
Trending Down -2% -2% -3.96% -11.64% ⚠️ Losses amplified 3x

💡 Key Insight: Decay Is Minimal in Trending Markets

Volatility decay primarily hurts in sideways, choppy markets. In strong trending markets (like summer 2025), the compounding effect actually helps leveraged ETFs. This is why TQQQ rose nearly 6,000% from 2010-2020 despite the "decay" warnings.

Why the "A Better QQQ" Design Mitigates Risk

Position % of Portfolio Has Decay Risk? Expense Ratio
TQQQ 12.5% (1/8) ⚠️ Yes 0.86%
7 Individual Stocks 87.5% (7/8) ✅ No 0%
Portfolio-Level Impact 100% Decay on only 12.5% ~0.11% blended

By holding 87.5% in regular stocks, the strategy avoids leveraged ETF decay on the majority of the portfolio. TQQQ provides a leverage "boost" while individual stocks provide stability.

The Bigger Risk: Drawdowns, Not Decay

The real danger with leveraged ETFs isn't daily decay — it's major drawdowns:

Drawdown Recovery Needed Historical Example
-33% +50% to break even —
-50% +100% to break even —
-75% +300% to break even —
-81% +426% to break even TQQQ in 2022 Bear Market

This is why the strategy has defensive modes — the UVXY hedge (triggered Sept 22), SQQQ positions, and BSV bonds are designed to protect against catastrophic drawdowns.

📌 Bottom Line on Holding TQQQ

Holding the "A Better QQQ" basket for extended periods is acceptable because:

  1. TQQQ is diluted to 12.5% — decay impacts only a small slice of the portfolio
  2. 87.5% in regular stocks — no leverage decay or extra expense ratios
  3. Trending markets minimize decay — 2025's bull run meant compounding worked in our favor
  4. Automatic escape hatches — strategy rotates to defensive positions when danger signals appear

The strategy would be much riskier if it held 100% TQQQ for months — but it intentionally doesn't. The diversified basket design is a key risk management feature.
